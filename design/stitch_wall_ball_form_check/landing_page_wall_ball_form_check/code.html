warning

A single camera can't judge everything.

Front-on angles

Cannot judge depth accurately. Hip crease and knee joint alignment require an unobstructed lateral perspective.

Target impacts

The ball hitting the target isn't tracked. Perspective tilt and wall occlusions make 2D edge contact ambiguous.

Occluded limbs

A hidden wrist or knee yields a "can't judge" call instead of guessing. We refuse to invent unseen coordinates.

Next: wearable motion and muscle sensors fill these gaps.

Integrity

Built from scratch, not a template.

The overlay you see on the video is ours. An open-source pose model (Google's MediaPipe) finds 33 body points in each frame; everything after that is built here: the skeleton overlay drawn frame-by-frame in sync with the video, the knee-line depth guide, rep detection from hip movement, the rule checks written from the rulebook, and the coaching summary. No AI model decides your verdicts — every call comes from a rule you can read.
